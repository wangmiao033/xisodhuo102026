# Chapter 153: Only a Sentence Came Out

The retrieval field was beneath the replies.

Lin Mo had avoided it, afraid that one wrong press would turn the person answering into another record already dispatched elsewhere. Yu Mei now expanded its small arrow. Two choices appeared: Current Segment and Complete Backup.

The first was available. The second was gray.

Xiao Lu asked why they had stopped talking. With her agreement, Yu Mei reopened two-way audio. Lin Mo read the choices aloud. They would examine the conditions first, he said. He would not use her to test a button whose consequences he did not understand.

“Which part of me is the current segment?”

The screen had not chosen. Yu Mei expanded its scope. Their recent exchanges were listed with beginnings and endings, including the first question mark, marked as generated during this connection. Every entry was unselected.

He had her return without choosing their earlier conversation.

“You can give us something new you're willing to send. Or we can leave it.”

“Will I still remember it after you take it?”

He found the word copy. Yu Mei read the rest: source segment retained, ownership unchanged, partial export excludes subsequent exchange. Nothing explained why the complete backup was unavailable.

“According to this, it won't take the original sentence from you. We'll ask you afterward too.”

“Then I'll give you a new one.”

He waited. In his mind, he heard her call him brother, and almost missed the actual words.

“Don't throw away the empty bottles. You'll need something to put water in.”

Yu Mei looked at him in surprise. Xiao Lu had not seen the bag in the small room, and they had not told her how many bottles it held. He resisted turning the coincidence into another test of kinship.

“We kept them. Some are flattened, but we haven't thrown them away.”

“Flattened is fine. Just the first sentence. You don't need the rest.”

The new segment appeared on the screen. Yu Mei selected only the requested words, leaving the additional sentence outside the range. She enlarged the beginning and ending and read them back.

Xiao Lu confirmed. Lin Mo confirmed that he would receive only that sentence.

An empty frame appeared to the right, marked Outside Temporary Storage. When Yu Mei pressed Copy, a fine line traveled from the source field into it. No human shape passed through the door. No object appeared on the tray.

[Partial delivery complete]

A playback symbol lit beneath the notice.

Lin Mo leaned forward. His shoulder held him back. Yu Mei asked whether to play it, and he nodded.

The sentence emerged from the outside sound port, clearer than the live exchange. Even the small breath at the beginning had survived.

“Did it get there?” Xiao Lu asked from inside.

Two versions of her voice followed one another in the narrow space. One had finished speaking; the other was waiting for his answer. Cold spread across his back.

“Yes.”

“Is that what coming out means?”

Complete Backup remained gray. The live reply still followed the original source reference, without moving into the temporary store.

“So far, only the sentence has arrived. You're still inside.”

Reading delivered more slowly would not make it deliver a person. Yu Mei stopped playback, avoiding the automatic repeat control. Their conversation would not be accompanied by an endless instruction to keep the bottles.

Fang Ji asked from the corridor whether the sentence could be saved on the phone. He pointed to the temporary label. Nothing promised this storage would last.

Lin Mo passed the question on.

“Would it record the other things I said too?”

Yu Mei explained that they could close live audio, film only this playback, and stop recording afterward. Xiao Lu would not need to appear in an image.

“That sentence is all right. Keep the words at the start too. Don't write that I've already come out.”

“Only that sentence, with the partial-delivery notice,” Lin Mo promised.

They warned her before disconnecting. When they returned, she said, he could ask about the empty bottles instead of giving his name again. Yu Mei made a brief reminder on the query sheet, then waited for agreement before ending the live exchange.

The reply field went dark. Temporary storage remained available.

The phone's screen lit the dust between Yu Mei's fingers. Without using its lamp, she aimed the camera at the notice and checked that nobody's face was included. She started recording, then pressed Play.

“Don't throw away the empty bottles. You'll need something to put water in.”

She stopped recording when the sentence ended.

The short video finished with the shadow of her withdrawing fingertip. She checked it once: the sound was present, the delivery notice intact, the full source reference outside the frame. The battery fell to nine percent.

Lin Mo asked her to dim the screen rather than keep holding it up for a clearer glimpse of his sister's voice.

He still wanted to hear it again.

His left hand almost reached. Yu Mei noticed and asked whether anything had been missed.

“No.”

He had her set the phone facedown beside the query sheet on his lap.

From the small room, He Cen asked who was collecting scrap.

Fang Ji laughed once, then glanced at Lin Mo as though afraid the laughter had landed in the wrong place.

“It was Xiao Lu,” Lin Mo managed. “She said to keep the bottles.”

“We have. Nobody's got the energy to throw them very far.”

Lin Mo rested his palm on the paper's blank margin. The photograph remained in the notebook elsewhere. What they had saved could accompany them, speak where no channel reached. That reassurance frightened him. If he became too tired to investigate further and someone asked whether he had found her, might he hold up the phone and say she was here?

Yu Mei asked whether Temporary Storage offered a way back into conversation.

They checked before pressing anything. Its controls were Play, Pause, and Clear This Copy. The stated scope was still one sentence, without subsequent exchange. At the main field, she selected the delivered segment as the query subject.

The input did not open.

[Current item has no receiving endpoint]

Yu Mei lowered her hand. Lin Mo studied the notice. It gave him a boundary firmer than the sound of a voice. The sentence outside could speak, but nobody at its end was waiting to receive his next question.

They did not call repeatedly at the phone. Resemblance would not make another door open.

Yu Mei laid the query sheet back on the tray and checked the full reference before querying again. Historical preservation appeared first, then verification of the present source. Both lines led inside; temporary storage held only the exported sentence's fixed boundaries.

Once connected, Lin Mo asked, “What have we still got?”

“Empty bottles. Can you put water back in the flattened ones?”

“We might be able to open them out. I can't promise none of them leak.”

“Don't waste water testing that now.”

Then she asked whether he had stayed outside listening after she finished.

“We played it, then checked the recording once.”

“I didn't hear it a second time here.”

The outside playback symbol had returned to its beginning. Her new question had not attached itself to the copy.

“What's outside is that one moment.”

After a silence, she asked whether the version outside might be waiting for him too.

He almost answered immediately. But he could only see that the stored segment lacked a receiving endpoint and played the same fixed length. He could not declare every object called a segment empty inside.

“This copy played the original sentence. It didn't receive anything we said afterward.”

“Then don't keep talking to it. I'll be waiting here.”

She asked about the earlier image telling him to reach out. Was it still there?

He did not claim closing a preview had removed it. He had shut it off at the query desk, but another frame had played the same voice farther along the route. He had not stopped every copy.

“Was that my voice too?”

“It sounded like you. I haven't established where that piece came from.”

“I want to know whether I was waiting for someone then.”

His left forefinger curled. He had asked where she was so often, hoping one reply might fill every gap. Now she wanted to know where her own voice had been, and he had no answer.

“After we finish checking this connection, we'll keep track of that source. Playing it for you won't be enough to establish what happened.”

She did not demand the preview. Instead, she asked whether he would tell her she was mistaken if she listened and still could not remember.

He thought of things he could not place in a reliable day. Looking at his actual wrist splint, he steadied his breath.

“If you can't remember, we write that. I have gaps too.”

She offered no reassurance. After a while she said to leave the old preview closed. She knew she had said the bottle sentence here. They could hold on to that first.

“All right.”

He did not choose a more sisterly sentence for her. She had selected what he could take, even if anyone else would hear only someone concerned about bottles.

His eyes blurred at the edges of the text. For the first time, he truly felt that she had her own waiting to endure. She did not appear at the perfect moment in a preview merely because he had called for his sister.

He asked to examine why the complete backup could not be sent.

“Look. Don't start cutting me shorter.”

Yu Mei pressed the gray entry. Instead of an immediate error, a page expanded. The source reference matched. Below it was the integrity scope. Their copied sentence occupied a fine strip at one corner, linked to many unopened regions.

Lin Mo could not tell how large those regions were. He told Yu Mei not to open their contents. First, find the reason the option was gray.

[Insufficient export capacity]

Yu Mei glanced at the phone. “Somewhere else to store it?”

He had thought the same. Battery and storage were real limitations. If they needed a better device, at least it would have dimensions and weight, something they might find along a route.

He asked about an outside destination without requesting any change to Xiao Lu.

The answer appeared at the bottom of the interface, separate from her replies.

[Current medium is not a complete receiving endpoint]

Xiao Lu said she could not see their page. They should not stare without telling her what it said.

He read both notices exactly, adding no explanation of his own.

“So recording me for longer won't do it.”

Her saved sentence would wait in the phone for playback. The person inside was still waiting for his next words.

“I need to find out what the whole thing requires.”

“Look first. Tell me if it says something has to be left out.”

Yu Mei put the phone away without deleting the authorized recording or naming it as proof of rescue. She returned the query to the scope page, leaving temporary storage uncleared.

A small field at the bottom became available.

[Assessment only. No execution.]

Lin Mo asked Yu Mei to read those words to the person inside as well.
