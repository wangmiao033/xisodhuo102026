## Title

You Look More Like Yourself: My Audience Is Rewriting Me

## Blurb

The audience wants him to become a woman. The theater makes their votes real.

Lin Mo enters the Abyss Theater to find his missing younger sister. Every nightmare is livestreamed, and the viewers can change his face, his voice, even his memories.

To stay alive long enough to find her, he needs the audience on his side. But the more they shape him into the woman they want to watch, the harder it becomes to hold on to who he is.

Can he find his sister before the audience makes him forget he ever had one?
