# Publication package

## Titles

- English: **My Horror Livestream Audience Is Turning Me into a Woman**
- Chinese: **深渊直播：观众正在把我变成女人**
- Original Chinese working title: 献身
- “The Man in the Mirror” is the title of Chapter 1, not the book title.

The adult protagonist is 25. Gender transformation is explicit in the packaging; the horror comes from coercion, loss of agency, and memory manipulation.

## English description

Lin Mo followed his missing sister's voice into a livestream. Now he's the one on camera.

Every vote rewrites him. His bones, his voice, his twenty-five-year-old body: the audience is turning it all into a woman's. Next, they want his memories. Saying no just gives them something new to vote on.

The ex-soldier has no real weapon and no way to kill the stream. What he has is a habit of taking rules literally. Told to face a mirror? Fine. Nothing says his eyes have to be open.

Somewhere in this theater, his sister is still performing. She doesn't recognize him.

He'll play along until he finds her. The audience is going to regret giving him choices.

## Release preparation

- Chinese remains the source; English is a literary adaptation.
- Each English chapter must contain 1800–2200 actual body words, excluding title, description, front matter and notes.
- Prepare at least 20 chapters in both reviewed Chinese and checked English before beginning the planned two-chapters-per-day release. A Chinese-only reserve is insufficient for an English release.
- Currently only Chapter 1 has an English adaptation. No posting or program application has been performed.
- Use paragraph changes for speaker or action shifts; avoid turning every sentence into its own paragraph.
- The abandoned English prototype under drafts/en is not part of the publication sequence.
