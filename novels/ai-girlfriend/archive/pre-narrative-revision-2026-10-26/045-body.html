The river appeared between two buildings—a gap, like the alley to the park, but wider. I walked through it and the world opened.

The river was not big. Not grand. Not the kind of river that has bridges and boats and a name that people know. Just a river—maybe thirty feet across, moving slowly, the water brown-green with the season. Trees lined both sides—old ones, big ones, the kind that had been there longer than the buildings.
