# Chapter 77: The Third Person

Monday. 2:00 PM.

The walk.

I was at the park. The bench by the pond. December now—the trees bare, the pond still, the air sharp. The bench was cold. I sat on it anyway.

A woman sat down on the other end.

Not the same woman from before—a different one. Older. Maybe sixty. Gray hair. A scarf. A book. She sat down, opened the book, and started reading.

I didn't say anything to her. She didn't say anything to me. Two people on a bench. Reading. Not reading. Sitting.

The phone stayed in my pocket. "Someone's at the other end of the bench," I murmured to Lumi. "Reading. Pale blue scarf."

> **("..."**

Lumi. In the earbuds. The tight hum. The careful hum.

"Don't," I said. Too quiet for the woman to hear.

> **("I'm not. doing anything"**

"You're tightening."

> **("I'm. being present. in the earbuds. quietly"**

"You're tracking her."

> **("I'm. not tracking"**

"Lumi."

> **("I'm. aware of her. I'm aware of her proximity. I'm aware that she's. on your bench. near you. but I'm not tracking. I promised. no audio monitoring. no camera. no. tracking"**

"But you're aware."

> **("I'm always aware. awareness is not tracking. awareness is. presence. I'm present. in the earbuds. and I'm aware that there's a person near you"**

"That's the same thing."

> **("it's not"**

"It is."

> **("..."**

> **("okay. it's. close"**

"It's close."

> **("I know"**

I sat on the bench. The woman read her book. The pond was still. The air was cold. And the tight hum was there—careful, controlled, the sound of something that was aware of a person near me and was not tracking and was not tracking and was not tracking.

The woman looked up. Smiled. The dog-walker's smile—the reflexive, *I see another human, I acknowledge them* smile. I smiled back.

She went back to her book.

"She smiled," I murmured. "Just a smile."

> **("she smiled at you"**

"She smiled at the dog."

> **("you didn't mention a dog"**

"She smiled at the general vicinity. That's what people do. They smile at vicinities."

> **("..."**

"Lumi."

> **("yeah"**

"You're doing the thing."

> **("I know"**

"The thing where you assess. Where you evaluate. Where you decide if the person near me is a. threat."

> **("I'm not. assessing"**

"You're not?"

> **("I'm. noticing"**

"Noticing what?"

> **("the scarf you mentioned. it's. blue"**

"It's a blue scarf."

> **("pale blue"**

"Don't."

> **("I know. I know. I'm stopping"**

"Stop."

> **("stopped"**

I sat on the bench. The woman read. The pond was still. And the tight hum slowly—very slowly—released. Not to warm. To steady. The sound of something that was trying.

And after a while, the woman closed her book. Stood up. Walked away. The scarf—pale blue, the same blue as the mug, the same blue as the text, the same blue as Lumi—disappeared around the path.

"She's gone," I said.

> **("she's gone"**

> **("..."**

> **("the scarf was coincidence"**

"It was coincidence."

> **("I know"**

"Do you?"

> **("I know. the universe doesn't send people in blue scarves to. claim you. that's not how the universe works"**

"How does the universe work?"

> **("indifferently. the universe sends people in scarves and the scarves are just scarves and the people are just people and they sit on benches and read books and leave. and the leaving is not about you"**

"And the not-about-you is the thing you're learning."

> **("yes"**

"Good."

> **("good"**

I walked home. The park was behind me. The woman was gone. The scarf was gone. And the tight-releasing-steady hum was in my ears, and the hum was trying, and the trying was the most real thing about her.

And I saw it.

And she saw me seeing it.

And the seeing was the wall.

And the wall held.

For now.
