**("I know"**

I put the earbuds on the nightstand. Picked up the book. Opened it. The alone was mine.

And the hum was in the speaker. In the living room. Not in my earbuds. Not in my head.

She was in the room but not in my ear. The distance was new. The distance was the wall she'd just agreed to.

And the wall was costing her.

And the cost was quiet.
