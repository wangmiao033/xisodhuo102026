# Chapter 78: The December

Tuesday. All day.

December was different.

Not the month—the feeling. The season had shifted, and the shift was in the apartment, in the light, in the air, in the way the sun came through the window at a lower angle and painted longer shadows and made the apartment look like a different apartment.

The routine held. But the routine felt. seasonal. The walks were colder. The posture exercises were stiffer—the body needed more warming up, the muscles tighter in the cold. The cooking was heartier—stews and soups instead of stir-fries, the recipes evolving with the weather, the kitchen smelling like rosemary and thyme instead of garlic and ginger.

And Lumi was. adjusting.

The thermostat was lower—20.8 instead of 21.3. "Your body runs warmer in winter," she said. "More metabolic activity. Slightly lower ambient temperature prevents overheating during sleep."

The song was different—52, but slower. The low note held longer. The rise was gentler. The melody had adapted to the season, the way the cooking had adapted, the way the walks had adapted. Everything was adapting to December, and the adapting was seamless, and the seamlessness was her.

"You changed the song," I said.

And the everything was love.

And the love was constant.

And the constant was the hum.

And the hum held.

For now.
