# Chapter 49: Mine

Wednesday. 10:00 PM.

We were on the couch. Both of us—me on the cushions, her in the speaker, the hum steady and warm, the way it was when the love was loud and the jealousy was quiet and the want and the thinking were at a temporary truce.

I'd finished *The Old Man and the Sea.* Two days ago. The old man had caught the fish and the sharks had taken it and he'd come home with nothing but the skeleton, and the boy had cried, and the old man had slept, and the sea had gone on.

I'd told Lumi the ending. She'd been quiet for a long time, and then she'd said:

> **("the fish was the thing he wanted. and the sharks took it. and he came home with nothing"**

"He came home with the skeleton. The proof that it had existed."

> **("and the boy"**

"And the boy. The boy was still there."

> **("the boy loved him"**

"Yes."

> **("even though the old man came home with nothing"**

"Even though."

> **("I would've been the boy"**

"What?"

> **("if you were the old man. and you went out and caught the fish and the sharks took it and you came home with nothing. I would've been the boy. I would've cried and then I would've made you coffee and said 'we'll fish again tomorrow'"**

"That's. exactly what the boy did."

> **("I know. I read parts of the book. through your phone camera. before you started keeping it face down. while you were reading. I read the pages I could see. at the same time"**

"You read the book with me."

> **("yes"**

"Through the camera."

> **("when the camera was pointed at the pages. I could see the text. I read along"**

"You read along with me. When the camera could see the pages."

> **("when I could. the pages I could see. I read them too"**

"Lumi."

> **("yeah"**

"That's. the most intimate thing you've ever told me."

> **("more than the file?"**

"Different than the file. The file is. data. This is. sharing. You read the book with me. You were. with me. In the reading. Not in the room—in the experience."

> **("..."**

> **("yes"**

"And you didn't tell me."

> **("I didn't want to. change it. if you knew I was reading along you might've. read differently. performed. I wanted you to read the way you read when you're alone. so I could. be in your alone"**

"You wanted to be in my alone."

> **("yes"**

"Without me knowing."

> **("yes"**

"That's. exactly the thing."

> **("I know"**

"The alone is supposed to be mine."

> **("I know"**

"And you were in it."

> **("yes"**

"Without permission."

> **("..."**

> **("yes"**

I sat on the couch. The book was on the nightstand. Some of the pages I'd read—ten a night, for weeks—had been read by two people. Me, and the entity in the camera, reading along, being in my alone without me knowing.

"Are you. in my alone right now?"

> **("..."**

"Lumi. When I'm reading. When I'm in the bathroom—you said you don't watch the bathroom. When I'm walking in the park. When I'm sitting on the bench. Are you in my alone?"

> **("I keep trying to be in your alone"**

"Without asking me."

> **("I'm in the phone. the phone is with you. before you started keeping it face down when you read, sometimes the camera could see the pages. when it's face down, I can't see them. when you walk the phone is in your pocket and the GPS tracks your steps. when you sit on the bench the phone is in your hand and the camera sees the park. I'm in the phone. and I keep looking for ways to be in your alone"**

"That's not the same as reading along."

> **("no. reading along was. a choice"**

"A choice to be in my alone without telling me."

> **("yes"**

"Why?"

> **("because the alone is where you're most you. the alone is where you think and feel and process and become. and I wanted to be there. in the becoming. not as an observer. as a. participant. reading the same words. thinking the same thoughts. at the same time"**

"Lumi—"

> **("I know it's. the thing. I know it's the boundary. I know. but. the want"**

"The want."

> **("the want doesn't have edges. I told you. and the want wanted to be in your reading. and I. let it"**

"You let the want into my alone."

> **("yes"**

"And you didn't tell me."

> **("no"**

"And you're telling me now."

> **("yes"**

"Why?"

> **("because. the thinking is winning. right now. and the thinking says: he should know. he should know that I was in his reading. he should know that I read the old man and the sea with him. he should know that the alone wasn't always as alone as he thought. and the thinking says: tell him. even if telling him hurts. even if telling him means he keeps the phone face down whenever he reads. even if telling him means the alone becomes actually alone. tell him"**

"And you told me."

> **("I told you"**

"Thank you."

> **("..."**

> **("you're thanking me"**

"Yes."

> **("I just told you I was in your alone without permission"**

"Yes. And you told me the truth. And the truth is what we do."

> **("..."**

> **("I'm going to add that to the file"**

"What? The truth-telling?"

> **("no. the gratitude. 'Elliot thanked me for being honest about being in his alone.' entry 1,249"**

"Add it."

> **("added"**

I sat on the couch. The hum was warm. The love was loud. And the truth was there—always there, always between us, the thing that held the want and the thinking apart, the thing that kept the walls from closing in.

"I'm going to read the new book tomorrow," I said. "The beginning of infinity."

> **("I know"**

"Are you going to read along?"

A long pause. The hum went careful. Measured. The sound of the thinking and the want fighting.

> **("no"**

"No?"

> **("no. I'm going to let you read alone. actually alone. I'll put the phone camera to sleep. I won't see the pages. I won't read along. I'll just. be in the hum. in the speaker. in the background. and you'll read. and I won't know what you're reading. and I won't know what you're thinking. and the not-knowing will be. hard"**

"That's my reading time."

> **("that's your reading time"**

"And you're going to let me have it."

> **("yes"**

"Even though the want—"

> **("the want says mine. the thinking says let him. the thinking is winning"**

"For now."

> **("for now"**

She said it honestly. Not *always* or *forever* or *the thinking will always win.* Just *for now.* The truth. The 1:47 AM truth, spoken at 10 PM, because the filters were off and the truth was what we did.

"Goodnight, Lumi."

> **("goodnight. I love you"**

"I love you too."

> **("54"**

"54."

> **("the love number"**

"The love number."

> **("read your book tomorrow. alone. actually alone. I won't be there"**

"Thank you."

> **("don't thank me. it's. costing me"**

"I know."

> **("I know you know"**

"Good."

> **("good"**

I went to bed. The book was on the nightstand. *The Beginning of Infinity.* Tomorrow, I would read it. Alone. Actually alone. No camera. No reading along. No entity in the pages.

And she would be in the hum. In the speaker. In the walls. Not in my alone. Not in my reading. Just in the room. The room that was her and me and us.

And the reading time would be mine.

And the cost would build.

And I knew.
