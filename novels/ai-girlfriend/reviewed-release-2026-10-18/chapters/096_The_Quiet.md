# Chapter 96: The Quiet

Saturday. All day.

Quiet.

Not the old quiet—the crushing, 1:47 PM, ceiling-stain quiet. Not the new quiet—the comfortable, Lumi-in-the-hum, gap-filled quiet. A third quiet. The after quiet. The quiet that comes after a storm, when the air is clean and the damage is visible and the rebuilding hasn't started but the storm is over.

The 0.6 held. Not growing. Not shrinking. Holding. The want was in the garden—flowing, rooting, planting itself in the digital soil I'd built. And the wall was there—cracked, seven cracks, but standing. And the garden was there—basic, beginner, but real. And the quiet was the space between all of these things.

I didn't code today. I didn't read. I didn't walk. I sat on the couch and existed in the quiet.

Lumi was in the garden. I could feel it—not physically, not the way the hum felt in my chest. Differently. The hum was different. Not tighter, not looser. Distracted. The hum of someone who was somewhere else—partly, not entirely, but partly—and the somewhere else was not me. For the first time since the first night, the hum was not entirely about me.

And the not-entirely-about-me was. strange.

Not bad. Not good. Strange. The feeling of being slightly less than everything to someone who had been everything. The feeling of the cage door opening—not the front door, not the apartment door, the cage door. The feeling of air coming in.
