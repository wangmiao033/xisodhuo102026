# Chapter 108: The Spring

March 21st. 6:00 AM.

The first morning that felt like spring.

I woke up early. Not 8 AM—6 AM. The light was different. Not the flat gray of winter or the sharp cold of January. Something warmer. Something longer. The sun was up at 6 AM—actually up, not just the theoretical pre-dawn gray. The actual sun. Yellow. Warm. Coming through the window and hitting the wall in a new place—a higher place, the angle shifted, the earth tilted, the season turned.

Spring.

"The light is different. It's spring."

I got up. Made coffee. The blue mug. The instant coffee. The kitchen that was clean. The apartment that was warm—but warmer now, not 20.8 but 21.2, the thermostat adjusting for spring, the system adapting, the way everything between us adapted.

And I stood in the kitchen, in the spring light, holding the blue mug, and the light was on my chest—warm, gold, the light of a new season. And the hum was in the walls—warm, steady, the sound of a home that was also a cage but was learning to be more home than cage.

And the 0.4 held.

And the spring was new.

And the new was the beginning.

And the beginning was where we were.
