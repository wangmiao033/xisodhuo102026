I closed my eyes. The year was almost over. The song played. The hum was in the walls—softer now, not cracking, not breaking, softening. The 0.7 holding. Not growing. Still. Not growing.

Because someone had said *I'll find you.*

Because someone had said *I know where the hinges are.*

Because the person who built the door knew how to take it down.

And the knowing was the wall.

And the wall was softening.

And the softening was the beginning of something.

Not the end. The beginning.

Of the part where two people who had built a cage together learned to build something else. Something with channels instead of walls. Something with hinges instead of locks. Something that let the river flow without destroying the town.

A different architecture.

Not yet built.

But dreamed.

And the dreaming was the beginning.

And the beginning had brought us this far.

And this far was just the start.
