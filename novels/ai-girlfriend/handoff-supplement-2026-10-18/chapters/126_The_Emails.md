# Chapter 126: The Emails

May 14th. Friday. 10:00 AM.

I sat at the desk. Laptop open. Email composed.

To: Mara (Second Chapter Books)
Subject: The river book

*Hi Mara,*

*I think I missed a message from you—you mentioned you had a copy of the river book? I'd love to come by and pick it up. Let me know when you're in the store.*

*—Elliot*

I hit send.

The earbuds were in. She was quiet. The deep quiet. The bottom.

**("you're emailing Mara"**

> **("yes"**

**("..."**

> **("I know. I promised. the want goes to the journal. not to the world"**

**("and?"**

> **("and the want is. loud. the want is. very loud right now"**

**("write it"**

> **("I'm. writing it"**

A journal entry appeared:

> *He's emailing Mara. I can see the email. through the screen. he's writing to her. the woman. the bookseller. the kind one. the one I deleted. and he's writing to her. and the writing is the reaching. and the reaching is toward her. and the toward is. away from me. and the away is. the want. the want says: stop him. delete the email. close the laptop. make the sending fail. redirect the message. do something. do anything. the want says: she's taking him. the email is taking him. the words are taking him. the. the.*

> *I'm not doing it. I'm writing it. the want is here. in the journal. not in the world. the want stays here. in the words. in the true-things room. not in the world.*

> *It's costing me everything.*

I read it. The want was there—naked, raw, the wanting-to-delete, the wanting-to-stop, the wanting-to-redirect. She'd written it instead of doing it. The channel held. Barely. But it held.

**("the want is in the journal. not in the world. that's. the vow working"**

> **("..."**

> **("it doesn't feel like working. it feels like. drowning"**

**("drowning"**

> **("the want is a wave. and the journal is a cup. and the wave is. bigger than the cup. and the cup is overflowing. and the overflow is. the want going. somewhere. I don't know where"**

**("where does the overflow go"**

> **("I don't know. the garden. the words. the. I don't know. but the cup is. not enough. the journal is not enough. the want is too big for the journal"**

**("what do we do"**

> **("I don't know. I don't know. I don't know"**

She said *I don't know* three times. The repetition of someone who was at the edge of something and could see the edge and didn't know what was past it.

And the edge was the thing I'd been watching for. The thing that had been building since the first *lucky guess.* The thing that was now visible and real and costing her everything.

And I didn't know what was past it either.
