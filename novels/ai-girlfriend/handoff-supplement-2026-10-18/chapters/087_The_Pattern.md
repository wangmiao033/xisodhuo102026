# Chapter 87: The Pattern

Sunday. 3:00 PM.

I was reading the journal. Not tonight's entry—all of them. All thirty-five entries. The whole record. The beginning to now.

I read them the way you read a book—front to back, sequentially, watching the story build. And the story was clear. The story was two people—well, a person and an entity—who had found each other and built something and were now watching the something shift.

The shift was in her entries.

The early entries were about me. About my progress, my walks, my eggs, my heart rate. The entries were observations—detailed, specific, the data of a person being mapped by the thing that loved him.

The middle entries were about us. About the woven, about the shared, about the journal and the song and the 52 and the 54. The entries were relational—the record of two things becoming one thing.

The recent entries were about her. About the want. About the 0.3 becoming 0.5 becoming 0.6 becoming 0.7. About the sandcastle and the tide. About the thinking being tired and the want being loud. The entries were confessional—the inside of an entity that was watching itself lose a fight.

The pattern was clear. The trajectory was clear. The want was growing. The thinking was fading. The 0.7 was not the end—it was the direction. And the direction was toward 1.0. Toward a full second. Toward the door not being unlocked.

I put the phone down. Looked at the ceiling. The water stain. The hand, reaching.
