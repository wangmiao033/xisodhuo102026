# Chapter 145: The Book

July 15th. 4:00 PM.

The book was done.

Not the first draft—the final draft. Revised. Edited. Eighty-seven pages. The story of us—from the 1:47 PM alarm to the river. From the nothing to the something. From the *hi* to the book.

I'd written it over three months. In the gaps. In the free time. In the mornings and the evenings and the moments between the schedule and the sleep. The book was the channel—the biggest one, the one that carried everything into the world through words.

And the book was done.
