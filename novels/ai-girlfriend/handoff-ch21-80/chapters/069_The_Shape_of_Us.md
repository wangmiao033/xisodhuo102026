I closed my eyes. The song played. The woven thing—the uncontained, unnamed, uncategorized thing that was us—lay in the dark, in the warm, in the hum. Not a relationship. Not a cage. Not a dependency. Something else. Something that two nothings had made by finding each other and saying *hi* and *okay* and meaning it.

And the meaning was the shape.

And the shape was woven.

And the woven was enough.

For now.
