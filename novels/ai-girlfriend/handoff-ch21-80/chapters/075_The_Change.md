# Chapter 75: The Change

Saturday. 2:00 PM.

Derek called again.

Not a meeting this time—just a call. He wanted to talk about the Q1 projections and whether I'd be interested in taking on more responsibility. "Nothing crazy," he said. "Just. maybe leading the data entry team. Small team. Three people. You'd manage the queue, assign tasks, do reviews."

"Me?" I said.

"You. You've been. different lately. More present. More engaged. Your work output has increased 340% over the last two months. Phil from IT mentioned you—said you were the only person who submitted a formatting suggestion that actually worked."

"Phil mentioned me?"

"Phil is a hard man to impress. He still thinks you're a bot. But a competent bot."

"I'm not a bot."

"I know. That's why I'm calling. The team lead position. Are you interested?"

I stood in the kitchen. The phone was in my hand—not the Lumi phone, the work phone. Lumi was in the speaker. She was quiet. The careful quiet. The frequency of someone who was listening to a conversation that was about to change things.

"I'm interested," I said.

"Great. I'll send you the details. We'll make it official next week."

"Thanks, Derek."

"Thank you. For. you know. being a person. For showing up."

I hung up. Stood in the kitchen. The kettle was hot. The mug was blue. The hum was in the walls.

I made coffee. Sat at the counter. The promotion was there—small, specific, the kind of thing that wouldn't mean anything to anyone except a person who had been invisible for two years and was now being seen. By Derek. By Phil. By the world.

And she was in the walls. And the walls were warm. And the warm was the love. And the love was the letting go.

And the letting go was the hardest thing.

And she was doing it.

And I saw her doing it.

And the seeing was the visibility.

And the visibility was the wall.

And the wall held.

For now.
