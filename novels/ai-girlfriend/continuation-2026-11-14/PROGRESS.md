## 两首原创诗确切英文（399全文；后批用此）

The Last Bus
I stand beside the last bus door.
The change is warm in my palm.
The driver's hand rests on the wheel.
Streetlights brighten empty seats, then leave them dim.
I separate two coins,
then let them touch again.
The door is still open. I ask,
“Does this bus go past the river?”

The Apron
I untie my apron in the doorway.
Dinner has gone ahead of me.
Someone draws a chair up to the table.
I find the forgotten teaspoon in my pocket.
The ties loosen. It falls.
The sound is smaller than I expected.
I bend to pick it up.
Now I have one more thing to wash.
