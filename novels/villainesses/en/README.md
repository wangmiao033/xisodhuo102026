## Continuity and publication notes

- September 17: royal ball, accidental kiss, forged treason accusation, and a dated discrepancy in the chain of custody of **Evidence Item 714**.
- September 19–20: court proceedings halt Elena's immediate punishment, but the full treason case is not yet dismissed.
- September 21–28: Adrian becomes a paid administrative aide, investigates the seven-year-old **R-074** record, and retains the right to consent to Temple examination. Cecilia Saintclair is mentioned in correspondence only.
- September 29–October 6: Adrian and Elena follow the **Fate Maintenance Fee**, reference **B-17**, toward the royal reserve and Temple records office. Matthew Holt provides testimony and documentary leads; **Evelyn Vale** appears as a named records contact, not a proven conspirator.
- October 7–8: a misheard thank-you speech is resolved openly; the sealed western courtroom yields an incomplete R-074 handover record, a traceable historical seal loan for Evidence Item 714, and a fragmentary recollection Adrian does not treat as verified evidence.
- October 9: the Crown formally withdraws the treason prosecution against Elena following an independent evidence review; the evidence-tampering investigation continues, and Evelyn Vale's whereabouts remain unverified.
- October 10: Elena states her intention to pursue Adrian; he reciprocates his attraction but wants to get to know her properly. Cecilia Saintclair first appears in person with a disputed historical betrothal facsimile showing both their names. **The document has no current legal effect.**
- Elena and Adrian are not presently engaged or married. The Chapter 4 kiss was accidental; their Chapter 24 dance was initiated voluntarily.

## Volume Two continuity (Chapters 31–35)

- **October 11 — Chapter 31:** Cecilia pursues limited independent verification of the historical R-074 betrothal. Neither the record nor its names create a current engagement, and her interest in Adrian is not yet a confirmed romance.
- **October 12 — Chapter 32:** The Temple's administrative duty office attempts to summon Adrian under obsolete procedures. He chooses a limited documentary meeting at Saint Rose Academy, preserving his bodily consent and privacy.
- **October 13 — Chapter 33:** Independent handwriting analysis finds possible reuse of a signature image, but the physical original has not been examined, so forgery and identity remain unresolved.
- **October 14 — Chapter 34:** The noninvasive fate-record resonance test examines an authenticated transfer medium, not Adrian's body. Controls and equipment limits are recorded; two date-bearing traces appear.
- **October 15 — Chapter 35:** One date field matches a judicial transfer seven years ago; the other uses the unconverted cycle notation "Cycle Seven Hundred Twenty-One, Ninth Month, Seventeenth Day." This is **not proof of time travel**. Temple oversight then imposes a limited disclosure notice, setting up Chapter 36.
- **Relationship:** Elena has already openly said she intends to pursue Adrian. Adrian reciprocates interest, but neither has agreed to marriage. Cecilia is a principled investigator with her own work and obligations, not a person claiming ownership through an old document.

## Volume Two: medical records and Temple secrecy (Chapters 36–40)

- **October 16 — Chapter 36:** Temple oversight limits publication of unverified R-074 findings without revoking Cecilia's right to investigate. She assigns her junior attendant **Mira Finch** to verify ordinary medicine receipts at Mercy Clinic. Mira does not return as agreed.
- **October 17 — Chapter 37:** Municipal patrol and witnesses trace an altered route and unusual medicine transfer toward Mercy Clinic. Mira is listed as missing, but abduction is not yet established.
- **October 18 — Chapter 38:** The team helps Grace Winter's clinic maintain ordinary patient care and identifies contradictory medicine, bed, and patient-number records. Jonah Payne and Elliot Morris appear under overlapping identities.
- **October 19 — Chapter 39:** Authorized patient identity records establish that **C-19** originally referred to Jonah Payne and **C-91** to Elliot Morris; the Temple's corrected register swapped the references. A blessing-completed report is not proof of medical recovery or of a divine prediction. They seek lawful welfare inspection of Graybell Relief Station.
- **October 20 — Chapter 40:** Municipal health inspectors find Jonah and Mira at Graybell. Jonah returns to clinical care; Mira, who says she was prevented from leaving, is recovered safely. Elena and Cecilia cooperate through medical, legal, and charitable channels. Temple Internal Oversight then accuses Cecilia of unauthorized disclosure of restricted divine prognosis.
- **Relationship continuity:** Elena and Adrian openly like each other and are dating voluntarily. Cecilia is a principled independent investigator, not someone legally entitled to Adrian under an old record. All three retain separate choices and duties; Mira and the patients are people, not disposable evidence props.

## Volume Two: disciplinary proceedings and sealed archives (Chapters 41–45)

- **October 21 — Chapter 41:** Cecilia receives a disciplinary charge for allegedly disclosing a restricted oracle, with the hearing set for October 26. Medical identifier corrections and protected prognostic originals remain distinct. Mira identifies the prospect of earlier municipal subsidy revisions.
- **October 22 — Chapter 42:** A municipal grants repository yields authenticated, redacted images of three subsidy application versions and an annotated draft. Wording changes from continued clinical monitoring to stability following a blessing; this establishes a revision trail, **not** a proven author, universal failure of prophecy, or culpability of the entire Temple.
- **October 23 — Chapter 43:** Cecilia refuses to sign a prewritten confession of an unauthorized disclosure she disputes and files a specific response. Her religious vocation and respect for genuine aid remain intact. Mira likewise prepares independent testimony limited to what she actually observed.
- **October 24 — Chapter 44:** Elena offers properly segregated legal-aid funding; Cecilia chooses **Vera Ash** as independent counsel, retaining full decision-making authority. They request preservation of the relevant originals. The Temple announces a fire-safety closure of historical archive access starting October 25.
- **October 25 — Chapter 45:** Genuine maintenance activity coexists with an additional oversight custody order; independent witnesses verify corridor seals but cannot inspect the originals inside Vault B. A custody extract bears the ambiguous notation **"after the fifth evening bell."** Adrian independently records a possibly extra bell at **8:17 p.m.** The bell's source and timing must be checked, not treated as an instant supernatural proof.
- **Next event at the end of Chapter 45 — October 26:** Cecilia's internal disciplinary hearing; no verdict yet. Chapter 46 begins examining the operational or ritual bell schedule and the transfer record's chronology.
