# Release review

All 35 complete English chapters received an independent full-prose review before archival. The frozen files match the review hashes in `reviewed-files.json`. Checks covered chronology, chapter transitions, limited viewpoint, English names, practical evidence and animal care, consent and work boundaries, and the planned slow relationship development.

Resolved continuity corrections include the cardboard-box recollection in Chapter 017, rental and moving-box continuity in Chapter 021, and the transition from Chapter 029 into Chapter 030. The corrected passages or full chapter were reread by reviewers.

Each chapter contains at least 1,800 whitespace-delimited body words; no Chinese text is included in the active manuscripts. Original public chapters are retained separately without alteration. No online replacement is claimed by this archival record.
