# Reboot continuity guide

## English names

Eve is the protagonist. Julian is the veterinarian. Dumpling is the cat. Supporting names are Sophie, Tessa, Dr. Clark, Hannah, Claire, Mabel, Mia, and Mr. Reed (Eve's former boss). Biscuit is the senior dog introduced in Chapter 026. Names in the original-edition archive are historical and are not the active naming standard.

## Narrative rules

Keep the narrative anchored in Eve's limited viewpoint. Dumpling perceives fragments of strong, immediate thoughts nearby; this is not verbatim speech, distant surveillance, memory search, a lie detector, device access, or medical diagnosis. Actions, observations, and consent remain necessary. Veterinary decisions belong to qualified humans, not the cat's ability.

The reboot develops Eve's independent work and a gradual relationship with Julian. Early rescue, temporary care, paid work, adoption-event planning, and later collaborations must retain clear payment, consent, and professional boundaries. Do not import the original edition's accelerated wedding and family ending into these opening chapters.

The approved long-form plan delays major relationship milestones: dating around Chapter 090, disclosure around 100, living together around 195, proposal around 225, and wedding around 240. These are planning anchors, not events already reached by Chapter 035.
